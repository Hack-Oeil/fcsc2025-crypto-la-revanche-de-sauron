Challenge #558

Sauron's Revenge

Humiliated by your first victory, Sauron has reinforced his security measures. Finding the idea of abandoning his ring-based encryption repugnant, he agreed with his minions on a ring change after each encryption. Fully confident in his new creation, he exchanges crucial messages with his most faithful servants.

The elves managed to intercept a mysterious writing, encrypted this time with a single ring. Will you be able to show Sauron once again that even the most powerful Dark Lord can be defeated?
